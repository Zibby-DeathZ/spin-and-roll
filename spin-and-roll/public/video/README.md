# Videos (optional)

- `intro.mp4`: your opening. Replaces the built-in studio ident and title. Any length;
  the storybook opens when it ends. 1920×1080, H.264 video with AAC audio.
- `jumpscare.mp4`: plays on the TV when a mimic springs, instead of the drawn maw.
  Keep it short (2 to 4 seconds).

Test both on the GM screen's Media tab.

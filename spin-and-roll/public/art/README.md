# Art (optional)

Pictures here replace the emoji on the TV map, the GM map, the phones' Here tab, the bag and the shops.
Anything without a picture keeps its emoji, so add them in any order.

    art/people/<id>.png     everyone the players can meet (shown in a circle)
    art/monsters/<id>.png   everything they can fight (shown in a circle)
    art/mimics/<id>.png     the Hungry Trunk and Painted Double after they spring
    art/items/<id>.png      items, shop goods, and items/wand.png for every wand
    art/chests/<id>.png     chests
    art/hazards/<id>.png    finale hazards

PNG, WebP or JPG all work. Square, 1024 × 1024 is plenty.
The full list of names, with a prompt for each, is in docs/art/art-prompts.md.
The GM screen's Media tab ticks off which files it finds.
